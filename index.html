I'm a Natural Language Processing (NLP) scientist and engineer, working on NLP and LLM applications in specialised domains such as defence and medicine, with a strong focus on multilinguality. I'm also drawn to the intersection of NLP/AI with the humanities and ethics.

Career

  1. 2024–present
    NLP scientist and engineer, Textgain Multilingual NLP for specialised domains: harmful-content detection (Calico) and bias-resilient intelligence analysis (SIMBA).
  2. 2020–2024
    Research fellow, ARC Training Centre in Cognitive Computing for Medical Technologies, University of Melbourne Automated quality assessment of medical evidence and COVID-SEE, with Karin Verspoor and Tim Baldwin.
  3. 2016–2020
    Postdoc, CLiPS, University of Antwerp Clinical NLP, machine reading comprehension and interpretability, with Walter Daelemans.
  4. 2012–2016
    PhD, Computational Linguistics, University of Groningen Thesis on word representations, advised by Gertjan van Noord and Ivan Titov.
